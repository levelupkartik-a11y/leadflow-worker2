Frequently Asked Questions

What types of health insurance plans do Anil Kumar Rajput and Star Health Insurance offer?

Anil Kumar Rajput, your trusted Star Health Insurance Advisor, provides a wide range of health insurance plans, catering to various needs and budgets. These plans can help you secure your family's health and financial stability in case of medical emergencies.

Can I purchase a health insurance plan if I already have a pre-existing medical condition?

Yes, Anil Kumar Rajput and Star Health Insurance can help you find a suitable health insurance plan, even if you have a pre-existing medical condition. Our expert advisor will guide you through the process and help you choose the best plan for your needs.

How do health insurance premiums work, and what factors affect them?

Your premium is the amount you pay for your health insurance plan. Premiums are typically influenced by factors such as age, health status, coverage options, and deductibles. By consulting with Anil Kumar Rajput, your Star Health Insurance Advisor, you'll gain a better understanding of how your premium is calculated and how to make informed decisions.

What happens if I need medical attention outside of India while my health insurance plan is active?

As a star health insurance policyholder, you typically have international coverage options available, which vary depending on your specific plan. If you require medical attention outside India, contact your insurance provider or Anil Kumar Rajput, your trusted advisor, to understand the steps and procedures for seeking treatment abroad.
