Our Heritage

Swiss Precision & Architectural Sanctuary

Founded in Montreux in 1998, NUMA was born from a singular vision: to reconcile high-performance clinical peptide science with the restorative tranquility of ancient botanical bathing rituals.

The Genesis

Where Clinical Science Meets Sensoric Grace

When Dr. Elena Rostova established our primary laboratory on the shores of Lake Geneva, she observed that conventional skincare often forced clients to choose between aggressive dermatological results and pleasant sensory experiences.

By developing our proprietary biomimetic peptide transport system alongside cold-pressed Alpine botanical nectars, NUMA eliminated this compromise. Today, our flagships in Paris, Zurich, and Tokyo stand as architectural sanctuaries where stress dissolves and cellular radiance is renewed.
