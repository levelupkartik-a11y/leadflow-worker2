Answers & Clarity

More Questions Answered

What if I have an urgent dental emergency after hours?

Existing patients of Aura Dental have access to our 24/7 on-call emergency line. Whether you experience severe dental pain, a chipped tooth, or a broken crown, call our main office number and follow the prompts to connect directly with the doctor on call for immediate guidance or emergency after-hours care.

Do you offer financing or payment plans for major cosmetic work?

Yes! We partner with CareCredit and Alphaeon Credit to offer 0% interest financing plans for up to 24 months for qualifying patients. This allows you to break up the investment of porcelain veneers, Invisalign, or biomimetic reconstructions into manageable monthly payments.

Can I schedule appointments for my entire family at the same time?

Absolutely. We offer "Family Block Scheduling" where parents and older teens can be seen simultaneously in adjacent treatment suites by Maya and our hygiene team, minimizing multiple trips to downtown Seattle.

Is parking validated at your downtown Seattle location?

Yes, we provide 100% complimentary parking validation for the underground garage located directly beneath 1200 5th Avenue. Simply bring your parking ticket to our reception desk upon arrival.
