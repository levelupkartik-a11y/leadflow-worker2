Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept dental insurance and direct billing?

Yes! We are in-network with most major PPO dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Regence, Premera, Cigna, MetLife, and Guardian. We handle all direct billing and insurance claims on your behalf. For uninsured patients, we offer our transparent Aura Patient Membership Plan.

What can I expect during my first visit to Aura?

Your initial comprehensive visit lasts approximately 90 minutes. It includes a complete set of low-radiation 3D digital x-rays, an intraoral camera tour, a periodontal health evaluation, a gentle Guided Biofilm Therapy cleaning, and a one-on-one consultation with your dentist to co-create your health roadmap.

How does biomimetic dentistry differ from traditional dentistry?

Biomimetic literally means "to mimic life." Traditional crowns require grinding down 70-80% of healthy tooth structure. Biomimetic dentistry uses advanced adhesive bonding and high-strength ceramics to repair only the damaged area—preserving your natural tooth, preventing root canals, and lasting decades longer.

What options do you offer for patients with severe dental anxiety?

We specialize in anxiety-free care. We offer three levels of sedation: nitrous oxide (laughing gas), oral conscious sedation pills, and IV twilight sedation administered by a certified specialist. Combined with our noise-canceling headphones, weighted blankets, and private suites, you can sleep comfortably through your entire procedure.
