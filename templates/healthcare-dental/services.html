Clinical Excellence & Mindfulness

Comprehensive Treatment Catalog

From gentle, warm-water hygiene to complex biomimetic ceramic reconstructions, every procedure is executed with meticulous surgical precision and anxiety-free comfort.

01 / Hygiene & Prevention

Guided Biofilm Therapy (GBT)

Say goodbye to painful metal scraping and freezing cold water. We utilize Swiss EMS AirFlow® technology—a state-of-the-art system that cleans teeth using a gentle, warmed spray of purified water and ultrafine erythritol powder.

  • ✦ Zero vibration, zero metal scraping, and zero sensitivity
  • ✦ Removes 100% of bacterial biofilm and coffee/wine stains
  • ✦ Safe around implants, veneers, and orthodontic brackets
  • ✦ Includes oral cancer screening & digital 3D AI scan
Schedule Your Cleaning
02 / Bespoke Aesthetics

Custom Porcelain Veneers & Whitening

Your smile should look distinctively yours—never chiclet-white or artificial. Dr. Rostova collaborates with master ceramists to hand-layer ultra-thin feldspathic porcelain veneers that mimic natural translucency and light reflection.

  • ✦ Digital Smile Design (DSD) preview before any treatment begins
  • ✦ Ultra-thin conservative prep preserving maximum tooth enamel
  • ✦ Custom shade matching tailored to your skin tone and facial symmetry
  • ✦ In-office Zoom® Laser Whitening for up to 8 shades lighter in 45 mins
Book Cosmetic Consultation
03 / Restorative Engineering

Biomimetic Same-Day Ceramics

Traditional crowns require drilling away up to 80% of a healthy tooth. As certified biomimetic specialists, we use advanced adhesive engineering and stress-reducing protocols to bond high-strength ceramic restorations directly to your tooth.

  • ✦ Prevents root canals by sealing teeth against bacterial leakage
  • ✦ In-house CAD/CAM 3D milling—receive permanent crowns in 2 hours
  • ✦ 100% metal-free, biocompatible zirconia and lithium disilicate
  • ✦ Mimics natural tooth flexibility to prevent future fractures
Learn About Biomimetic Care
04 / Clear Orthodontics

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Achieve a perfectly aligned, healthy bite without metal brackets or wires. Using our iTero Element™ 3D scanner, we capture a digital impression in 60 seconds and simulate your new smile instantly.

  • ✦ Virtually invisible, removable clear aligner trays
  • ✦ SmartTrack® material engineered for faster, more comfortable movement
  • ✦ Virtual check-ins via mobile app reducing required office visits by 50%
  • ✦ Includes complimentary professional whitening gel during treatment
Schedule 3D Scan & Simulation
05 / Comfort Sanctuary

Gentle Sedation & Pain-Free Care

We believe dental fear should never stand between you and optimal health. Whether you have dental phobia, a sensitive gag reflex, or require complex procedures, our dedicated sedation suites ensure profound relaxation.

  • ✦ **Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)**: Fast-acting mild relaxation; drive home safely immediately after treatment
  • ✦ **Oral Conscious Sedation**: Take a calming pill prior to your visit; snooze comfortably through your appointment
  • ✦ **IV Twilight Sedation**: Administered by our certified specialist; wake up with zero memory of the procedure
  • ✦ Spa amenities: Bose noise-canceling headphones, weighted blankets, aromatherapy
