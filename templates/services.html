Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect during my first sanctuary appointment? +

We recommend arriving 20 minutes prior to your scheduled time. You will be welcomed with our signature chilled organic herbal tea and guided to our relaxation lounge before undergoing your complimentary 15-minute multi-spectral dermal diagnostic scan.

Can I combine clinical facial treatments with body massages? +

Absolutely. Our master aestheticians and holistic massage therapists work in tandem. We frequently curate bespoke 2.5-hour immersion rituals that pair our Lymphatic Body Wrap with the Cellular Infusion Facial.

Are all NUMA skincare formulations pregnancy-safe? +

Over 90% of our botanical boutique formulations are entirely pregnancy and nursing safe, utilizing bio-mimetic bakuchiol instead of harsh retinoids. During your consultation, your therapist will tailor your protocol to ensure complete maternal safety and comfort.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling etiquette? +

Because our treatment rooms and master therapists are reserved exclusively for you, we respectfully request a 24-hour notice for any cancellations or schedule modifications to avoid a 50% reservation fee.
