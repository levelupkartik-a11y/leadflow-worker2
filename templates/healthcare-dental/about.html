Our Origin & Vision

The Sanctuary Story

Founded on a radical premise: What if dental care felt as restorative to your human spirit as it does to your physical health?

Why We Exist

Redefining an Industry

For over a century, traditional dentistry has been plagued by a clinical, sterile atmosphere that induces anxiety in over 60% of adults. Furthermore, aggressive drill-and-fill protocols often sacrifice healthy tooth structure for quick fixes.

In 2018, Dr. Elena Rostova and Dr. Marcus Vance came together in Seattle to build Aura—a sanctuary where high-tech biomimetic precision meets profound human compassion. Here, we don't just treat teeth; we care for the whole person behind the smile.

Sensory Architecture

Designed for Tranquility

Our Care Team

World-Class Clinical Specialists

Founder & Lead Cosmetic Specialist

Dr. Elena Rostova, DDS

Dr. Rostova received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), where she graduated top of her clinical class. She is an accredited member of the Academy of Biomimetic Dentistry and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD).

When she isn't lecturing nationally on conservative porcelain bonding techniques, Elena enjoys sailing on Puget Sound and practicing mindfulness meditation.

Director of Surgery & Sedation

Dr. Marcus Vance, DMD

Dr. Vance earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Harvard School of Dental Medicine, followed by a 3-year surgical residency in Periodontics and Implantology at the University of Washington. He holds mastership certification in IV Conscious Sedation.

Marcus is dedicated to helping patients overcome severe dental phobia. In his free time, he coaches youth soccer and volunteers with Seattle's free dental outreach clinics.

Lead Guided Biofilm Hygienist

Maya Lin, RDH, BS

With over 10 years in periodontal hygiene, Maya is certified in Swiss EMS AirFlow® Guided Biofilm Therapy. Her gentle touch and passion for patient education make every cleaning a relaxing spa-like experience.

Patient Concierge & Anxiety Specialist

Chloe Bennet

From greeting you with warm tea to assisting with direct insurance billing and scheduling, Chloe ensures your journey through our sanctuary is seamless, transparent, and completely stress-free.

Clinical Innovation

Advanced Technology Suite

We invest heavily in cutting-edge diagnostic technology to make your care faster, safer, and completely comfortable.

3D Digital Scanning

iTero Element™ 5D Plus

No more gagging on messy putty impressions. Our optical 3D scanner captures 6,000 images per second to create a flawless digital model of your teeth in under a minute.

Low-Radiation Imaging

3D Cone Beam CT (CBCT)

Provides a 360-degree 3D anatomical map of your jawbone, nerves, and sinus cavities with 80% less radiation than traditional medical CT scans for ultra-safe implant surgery.

Same-Day Milling

CEREC® CAD/CAM Robotics

Our in-house robotic milling station carves custom, high-strength ceramic crowns and inlays from solid blocks of biocompatible porcelain while you relax in our lounge.
