Frequently Asked Questions

What types of diagnostic tests do you offer at Central Clinical Lab?

Our lab offers a wide range of diagnostic tests, including blood tests, urine tests, imaging tests, and more. We also specialize in various niche tests and screenings such as histopathology, clinical pathology, and molecular diagnostics.

How do I prepare for my diagnostic test at Central Clinical Lab?

Please follow proper instructions provided by our staff or the doctor who ordered the test. Typically, this includes fasting or not eating/drinking certain things beforehand. Our staff will guide you on what specific preparation is required for your test.

What are the operating hours for Central Clinical Lab in Sector 17-C, Chandigarh?

Our lab is open from Tuesday to Sunday, 8:30 AM to 7:30 PM, and Monday from 8:30 AM to 7:30 PM. We are closed on public holidays and may have occasional special closures, so it's best to call ahead to confirm our hours before planning your visit.

What if I need urgent medical attention or results outside your operating hours?

For urgent medical needs outside our hours, we recommend visiting the nearest emergency room or contacting your family doctor for guidance. Meanwhile, our lab remains available to provide urgent test results during our regular operating hours, and our team will do their best to expedite your test result when possible.
